Account & Data Deletion Request

In accordance with Google Play developer policies and privacy standards, users of SipLumo have complete control over their personal information and data. You can delete your account and all associated hydration data at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Fastest)

Immediate 1-Tap Permanent Deletion:

  1. Open the SipLumo app on your device.
  2. Tap on the Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account & Cloud Sync or Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap "Delete Account & Clear All Data".
  5. Confirm your decision. All local data and cloud-synced records will be permanently erased immediately.

Method 2: Web-Based Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your mobile device, submit the deletion request form below. Our support team will permanently delete your cloud account and associated data within 24 to 48 hours.

✓ Your deletion request has been submitted successfully. Your account and data will be permanently wiped from our databases.

What Data is Deleted?

When you delete your account or submit a deletion request, the following information is permanently purged:

  • Personal Profile Data: Name, email address, weight, daily hydration targets, and custom drink preferences.
  • Hydration & Intake Records: All logged fluid timestamps, beverage types, volumes, and historical streaks.
  • Cloud & Database Records: Your Firebase Authentication user account and all Firestore synced documents.
  • Reminder Settings: Notification intervals, quiet hours, and scheduling preferences.

Data Retention Policy

We do not retain any personal hydration records or user identifiable information after account deletion. All backups containing user data expire and are purged in full compliance with GDPR and Google Play Data Safety standards.
