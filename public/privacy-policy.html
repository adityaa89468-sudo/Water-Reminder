Privacy Policy

Application: SipLumo - Water Reminder Package Name: com.aditya_production_water_reminder.app Effective Date: September 2, 2026

At SipLumo - Water Reminder ("we", "our", or "the App"), built and maintained by Aditya, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details the exact types of information we collect, how that information is utilized, stored, and shared, and the rights you retain over your data, in full adherence to the Google Play Developer Program Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and applicable international privacy laws.

Key Summary: SipLumo is architected as an offline-first utility. By default, all your hydration logs, daily targets, and schedule settings are stored locally on your device. We do not sell or broker your personal information. Optional cloud backup is provided via Google Firebase, and banner advertising is served via Google AdMob in accordance with user consent.

1. Information We Collect and Process

We only collect data that is strictly necessary to calculate personalized wellness hydration recommendations, send timely hydration reminders, and sustain our free service.

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Physical & Wellness Parameters: Body weight, height, approximate age bracket, and biological sex. These metrics are used strictly to calculate your baseline fluid intake guidelines (~30 mL/kg reference calculation) based on standard European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) references.
  • Daily Routine & Schedule: Wake-up time, bedtime, and quiet hours. These are used locally by the app's notification scheduler to ensure you are never disturbed while sleeping.
  • Environmental & Lifestyle Context: Activity level (low, moderate, high), climate conditions (cool, normal, hot), and optional flags for pregnancy or breastfeeding. These provide contextual adjustments to baseline fluid targets.
  • Hydration Intake Logs: Timestamps of fluid consumption, volume (in mL or oz), beverage categories (water, infused water, tea, electrolytes, mineral water, etc.), and optional user notes.
  • Special Health Considerations (Self-Reported): Indications of fluid restrictions or kidney, heart, or liver conditions, used solely to display prominent advisory banners prompting adherence to physician-prescribed limits.

B. Information Collected Automatically (AdMob & Diagnostics)

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): In accordance with Google Play Policy, the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) accesses the Android Advertising ID to serve banner advertisements, measure ad effectiveness, limit ad frequency, and prevent fraudulent clicks. You can reset or delete your AAID at any time in Android OS Settings.
  • Non-Personal Device Diagnostics: Coarse technical data such as operating system version, device model, language preference, and app crash logs may be processed anonymously by Google Play services to ensure app stability.

C. Optional Account Information (Cloud Sync)

If you optionally choose to sign in via Google Authentication to back up your progress across devices, we collect your email address, display name, and a unique Firebase Authentication User ID (UID). You can use the app indefinitely in Guest / Offline mode without providing any account information.

2. Android Operating System Permissions

SipLumo requests only the minimum permissions required for operation on Android devices:

Permission Purpose & Functional Need
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Required on Android 13 (API 33) and above to deliver scheduled water drinking reminders during your active hours.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Allows the app to reschedule your local notification timers automatically whenever your Android phone restarts.
WAKE_LOCK Allows the local alarm manager to briefly awaken the device to trigger the notification chime or vibration at scheduled times.
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Required to load banner ads via Google AdMob and synchronize your hydration data with Firebase Firestore (if cloud backup is enabled).
AD_ID Required by Google Play Services for apps integrating Google AdMob to access the Advertising ID for ad fraud prevention and ad serving.

4. User Consent & Ad Personalization (GDPR / UMP)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, SipLumo implements Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. On the first launch, eligible users are presented with a consent form to choose between personalized ads, non-personalized ads, or ad partners.

Changing Your Consent: You can review or modify your advertising consent settings at any time within the app by navigating to Settings → Privacy & Legal → Ad Privacy Preferences.

5. Data Security and Encryption

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app and external services (Google Firebase, Google AdMob) are strictly encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2/1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Isolated Cloud Data: If you use cloud sync, your data in Google Firestore is protected by strict security rules allowing read and write access solely to your authenticated Firebase UID.
  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, lease, rent, or trade your personal wellness information, body metrics, or drinking logs to any third-party data broker or marketer.

6. Account and Data Deletion Rights (Google Play Compliance)

We respect your right to completely delete your data at any time. In full compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, you have two simple ways to exercise this right:

  1. Immediate In-App Deletion: Open the App → Go to Settings → Select "Delete Account & Clear All Data". This instantly wipes all local logs, user profile metrics, notification timers, streak records, and cloud Firestore entries permanently.
  2. Web-Based Deletion Portal (No App Required): If you have uninstalled the app or lost access to your device, you can request permanent deletion through our public web portal at:
    https://siplumo.app/delete-account (or /delete-account). Remote deletion requests are verified and processed within 24 to 48 hours.

7. Health & Medical Policy Disclaimer

Google Play Health Policy Notice: SipLumo is NOT a Medical Device.
SipLumo is designed exclusively as an informational general wellness reminder app for healthy individuals. The calculated targets (~30 mL/kg standard) are general approximations. SipLumo does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any illness, kidney condition, cardiovascular disease, or dehydration-related pathology. If you have been advised by a physician or nephrologist to restrict fluid intake, you must strictly follow your physician's clinical instructions rather than the app's estimates.

8. Children's Privacy (Target Audience 13+)

SipLumo is intended for general audiences and individuals aged 13 and older (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information without parental consent, we will promptly delete that information from our records.
